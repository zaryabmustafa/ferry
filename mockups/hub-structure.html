What changes

  • The sidebar and the page are two rounded panels on the window background. The version number sits at the bottom of the sidebar, and Settings shows "Update" there when one is waiting.
  • Every page header is the title on the left and that page's actions on the right: Pause, Add folder, Test connection and Save, Refresh, Save settings. Save buttons move from the bottom of forms into the header.
  • Tabs under the title where a page has views: Folders (All, Carrying, Off), Locations (one tab per server, with a warning mark when one needs a look), Activity (Waiting for your OK, Recent).
  • Small uppercase labels over each section. Some carry an action on the right, like Remember this network or Manage folders.
  • One card per folder, network and held delete: a name with secondary text, the paths, and chips for state. Rare actions (edit, move, remove, forget, trust a VPN) go into a Manage menu instead of a row of buttons.
  • Unchanged: the first-run setup steps, the tray flyout, colors, type and controls (DESIGN.md).
