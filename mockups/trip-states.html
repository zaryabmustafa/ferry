How to read the bars

Each bar has a label above it saying what it measures, so an empty bar can't be mistaken for no progress.

On the laptop

Amber, one segment per folder, out of the laptop's 350 GB. The square next to each folder matches its segment.

Unloaded this trip

Hatched while unloading: space already freed on the laptop. Amber shrinks as Hive takes the files.

At Hive

Gray, out of the folder's full size at Studio. Shown when the laptop is empty and Hive isn't caught up.

What the engine has to send

Most of this comes from what Status already sends. Two things need a small addition.

  • Unloaded this tripNew Status::trip_bytes: how much was on board when unloading started, so the panel can say "286 of 350 GB unloaded".
  • Which folder is activeNew PairStatus::active, so the row being copied says "Loading" or "Unloading" instead of the panel guessing from step.
  • Loaded, On the wayNo engine change: the UI already knows where the laptop is and how much it carries. Today these show "Idle" and "Waiting: not on a known network".
  • Hive is 90% caught upNo engine change: each folder's share at Hive (destination_percent) weighted by its size at Studio (source_bytes).
